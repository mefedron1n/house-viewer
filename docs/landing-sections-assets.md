Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: before-construction image for an architectural website comparison slider. Edit the attached furnished kitchen photograph into the exact same room before interior finishing. Preserve the exact camera position, field of view, perspective, image framing and 16:9 composition, wall and ceiling edges, opening at the back, and the precise position and size of the back window. Remove all furniture, dining table, chairs, cabinets, appliances, TV, curtains, decorative objects, plants and light fixtures. Reveal bare clean grey concrete walls and ceiling with subtle formwork marks, unfinished grey screed floor, discreet electrical outlet cutouts and a few short hanging electrical cables in place of ceiling lights. Preserve all architectural geometry. Natural cool daylight from the existing back window, realistic construction photography, subdued grey stone palette, no people, tools, text, labels, logos, borders or watermarks. One full-frame image, not a collage or a split comparison.
